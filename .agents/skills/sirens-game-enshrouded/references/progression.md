# Progression

**Two skill points per level.** Experience comes from defeating enemies,
completing quests, and Experience Scrolls. **Maximum character level is 45, for
184 skill points across a run.**

Points also come from the world, once each: **one per Shroud Root felled, three
per Elixir Well cleared.** A world respawning after a server restart does not
restore those, so a player levels from each one exactly once.

**Four trees:** Core, available whatever the build, plus Ranger for ranged and
survival, Mage for magic, and Warrior for melee. The Flame Altar resets the tree
at one Rune per point ([flame-altar.md](flame-altar.md)).

**Six attributes, each per level:** Constitution +50 Health, Spirit +20 Mana,
Endurance +10 Stamina, Strength +5% melee damage, Dexterity +5% ranged damage,
Intelligence +5% magic damage.

**Vitals start at 100 each.** Stamina covers sprinting, double jumping, and
blocking, regenerating at 8 per second after a 0.5 second delay. Mana regenerates
on its own.
