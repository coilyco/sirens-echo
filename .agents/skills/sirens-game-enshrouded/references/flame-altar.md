# The Flame Altar

The altar is the key to any base. It makes building and terrain reshaping
permanent inside its area, carries the Strengthen The Flame upgrades, **resets
the skill tree at one Rune per point**, and serves as a fast travel point and a
heat source.

**Nine levels.** Base area is 40x40x40 at level 1, 80 at 2, 120 at 3, and
160x160x160 from level 4, which is the maximum and does not grow further.

**Shroud time rises one minute per level**, from 5 minutes at level 1 to 13
minutes at level 9. Character attribute bonuses start at level 2 and rise by one
per level, reaching +8 at level 9. Water source capacity climbs steeply across
the same ladder, from 1 in the low levels to 50 at level 9.

The levels past 4 buy shroud time, attributes, and water rather than more room
to build, which is the thing members are usually asking when they ask whether
another upgrade is worth it.
