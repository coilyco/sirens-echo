# The Shroud

Entering an enshrouded area starts a timer, because the player's inner Flame
protects them from the Shroud but not indefinitely. **Red fog drains the timer
much faster than ordinary white fog.** When it runs out the Shroud consumes the
player.

Time comes back from standing at a respawn point, which restores it fully, and
from canisters and potions, which restore an amount the wiki does not state.
The Flame Altar raises the maximum, one minute per level: read
[flame-altar.md](flame-altar.md) for the ladder. Some armor **lowers** the
maximum rather than raising it, the Guard of the North set among it.

**Shroud Roots sit at the bottom of Elixir Wells.** Felling one grants skill
points to every nearby Flameborn, an axe being the effective tool, and the
descent is defended.

**The wiki marks this page a stub.** So the timer's base duration, what a
canister restores, and the death and item-recovery rules are not documented
there and are unknown here. Say so rather than estimating, since a number
invented for this mechanic is one a member would act on underground.
