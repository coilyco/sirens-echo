## The official Enshrouded wiki

`enshrouded.wiki.gg` is the official wiki, player-written and player-maintained
rather than published by the developer, which is what the wiki says of itself.
It is the reference a member can open, not an authority on this server. Content
can change after this review, so treat a linked page as the current reference
rather than as a quotation.

## What this file deliberately omits

**No live-data surface exists for Enshrouded**, so there is no map, server,
or market page reading the Sirens world. Nothing listed here reads the running
world, and a link that appears to is one to refuse.

The developer's own support articles on dedicated-server configuration were not
reachable for review from here, so **they are not approved** and must not be
written even though they exist.
