# Survivors and what they unlock

A rescued craftsperson is what turns a recipe from unavailable into craftable, so
"how do I make this" is usually "which survivor have you found".

* **Oswald Anders**, blacksmith - melee armor and weapons, through the charcoal
  kiln, forge, and smelter.
* **Balthazar**, alchemist - magical armor and potions, through the mortar,
  alchemy station, and laboratory.
* **Cade Hawthorn**, carpenter - wooden construction and decoration, through the
  kiln and table saw.
* **Athalan Skree**, hunter - ranged armor and survival gear, through the drying
  rack and tanning station.
* **Emily Fray**, farmer - cooking, crops, and animal husbandry, through the
  seedbed, cooking station, and oven.

Beyond those five: **Alden Crowley** the collector for dark furniture and special
foods, **Valory** the bard for instruments, **Elio Ricci** the barber for
appearance, **Captain Melville Fontane** the fisher for fishing gear, and
**Mei-Yin Chen** for Lunar New Year seasonal items.
