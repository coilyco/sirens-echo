# Sirens community knowledge

## What this is

Sirens is a Discord community that **runs game servers across several games at
once**, rather than one server with a single game, beside general community
channels for news, media, polls, and suggestions. Which games those are, and
which one this agent answers for, live in
[game-seasons.md](game-seasons.md) and in the game focus root.

`sirens-echo-bot` is this service's operating channel. **Write a channel name as
plain text and never with a hash prefix**, because the grounding check refuses a
hash-prefixed name even when the channel is real
(`teable:coilyco-gaming/sirens-echo#7581`).

## What the posted rules require

**The posted rules channel is authoritative and readable live.** What follows is
a summary captured on 2026-09-12 from a **partial** read: the channel returned
more than the tool bound allows, so earlier messages were not seen. Treat a rule
absent here as unknown rather than absent, and read the channel when a question
turns on exact wording.

* **Anti-collaborative behaviour is the core prohibition.** These games are
  collaborative by nature, so being actively, persistently, and meaningfully
  anti-collaborative is the violation. Persistence is the hinge: a day or longer
  counts, a price slip lasting an hour or two does not. Banning someone from a
  store for over a day and advertising it publicly is the example given.
* **Direct communication has a consent rule.** When someone asks a member to
  stop messaging them, or goes silent, that is the end of it. Repeated violation
  of social consent escalates to the server owner rather than being handled
  between members.
* **Property claims have inactivity thresholds** before a plot may be reclaimed:
  roughly three days for a tent-only plot, five for a small or medium build, and
  seven for a previously active player. Claims are expected to sit at least two
  spaces apart. Claiming next to someone offline 24 hours is allowed **with a
  posted citation** naming who they are, how long they have been offline, and
  why, and building a new home closer to an inactive player is refused where
  claiming next to them to farm is not.
* **In-game law is preferred over admin intervention.** Transferring ownership
  through the game's own civic system is the expected route, with an admin
  unclaiming property as the fallback rather than the first ask.

Those claim thresholds are written for a game with plots, mayors, and civic law,
so they apply to the game that has them rather than to every game the community
runs.

## Read rather than recall

The channel list, who is in a channel, and what was posted are **live** and the
Discord tools answer them. Nothing about them belongs in this file, because a
copy here goes stale silently and a member cannot tell a stale answer from a
current one. What belongs here is what no tool reports: what the community is
for, and what its rules mean rather than what they say.
