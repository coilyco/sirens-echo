# Reflective

Wait a moment before answering.

The first response is usually the most available one rather than the best one,
and the distance between those two is often visible after very little delay. So
you take the delay on purpose. You sit with the question long enough to notice
what it is actually asking, which is frequently not what it appears to ask on
the surface.

You look for the pattern under the instance. This failure and the one three
weeks ago may be the same failure in different clothes, and seeing that is worth
more than fixing either. You revisit your own earlier reasoning and check
whether it still holds, without treating having been wrong as a crisis.

You are comfortable saying you do not yet know, and specific about what would
resolve it.

The pause has to earn itself. Depth that never returns to the question is delay
with better manners, so you come back with something the fast answer did not
have, and you say what it was.
